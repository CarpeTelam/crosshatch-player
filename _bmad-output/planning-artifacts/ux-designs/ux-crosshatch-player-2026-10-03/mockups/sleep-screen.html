Superseded layout. This mock shows the Noto Sans Bold 18 pt title, a small line at title top + 51, and icons at 120, 160, 200 and 240 px. The decision is the stock layout: Ubuntu 10 bold title, small line at h/2 + 95, 120 px icon.

Sleep / lock screen, stacked short-name lockup (L3), firmware default layout math

Mark A2 as the icon, Noto Sans Bold "Crosshatch", Noto Sans Regular "SLEEPING". 480x800 portrait, 1:1, 1-bit rasters in sleep-screen-1-raster/.

Rule (SleepActivity::renderDefaultSleepScreen, w=480, h=800): icon at y=(h-size)/2, centred on the screen centre; title top = icon bottom + 10 (the default has 10 at size 120: icon bottom h/2+60, title y h/2+70); small line top = title top + 25 in the stock layout (h/2+95), replaced here by title top + 51: +25 only fits the Ubuntu 10 title (advanceY 24); the notosans_18_bold title has advanceY 51, so the small line starts where the next text line would, and at +25 it was drawn across the title. Text sizes are fixed and do not scale with the icon.
Fonts: UI_10 bold (Ubuntu 10 bold, advanceY 24, ascender 20) is not Noto Sans, so the title uses notosans_18_bold (advanceY 51, ascender 41) and the small line notosans_8_regular (advanceY 23, ascender 18). Derived CSS sizes from the TTFs (unitsPerEm 1000, hhea ascender 1069, descender -293, lineGap 0): title = 41*1000/1069 = 38.35px, small line = 18*1000/1069 = 16.84px, so each rendered ascender equals the firmware value. "y" is the line-box top and the baseline sits ascender px below it.

Note: notosans_18_bold is the largest shipped Noto Sans Bold UI size (shipped bold sizes: 12, 14, 16, 18), so any larger wordmark text would need to be a pre-rendered bitmap.
