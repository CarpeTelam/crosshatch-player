Wordmark lockups on mark A2

3 lockups (L3 stacked has a three-line variant L3c in two sizings) x 2 firmware typefaces. Text capitalised exactly as given. Mark A2 unchanged. Fonts: NotoSans-Bold.ttf and NotoSerif-Bold.ttf from lib/EpdFont/builtinFonts/source, subset and embedded.

Screens: x4pro and sticky are both 800x480 panels (FreeInkDisplay DISPLAY_WIDTH 800 x DISPLAY_HEIGHT 480; sticky: 3.97" 800x480 SSD1677 per platformio.ini). SleepActivity sets Portrait, so the logical canvas is 480 x 800. Current boot/sleep layout: Logo120 at ((w-120)/2, (h-120)/2); title text at h/2+70 (UI_10 bold), "Sleeping" at h/2+95 (small). Default sleep screen is inverted to dark unless Light is chosen.
