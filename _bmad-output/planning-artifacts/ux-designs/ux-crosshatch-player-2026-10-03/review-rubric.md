# Spine Pair Review — crosshatch brand mark

Paths: `DESIGN.md` = D, `EXPERIENCE.md` = E, `.memlog.md` = M (line numbers are the files' own).

## Overall verdict
The pair is a sound, buildable contract for an identity-only spec. Every memlog decision I checked is present and none is contradicted: C2 final, Noto Sans Bold for the wordmark only, Ubuntu 10 bold device title, stock 120 px layout, 60% "Player", native 128 px icon, spacing rule, short/stacked/avatar placements, and the default icon not being an icon-library name. All contrast figures, luminances and the mark geometry recompute correctly. The weak spots are not in the decisions but in what is left unspecified or stated wrongly: the lockups (the main website deliverable) have no text-to-mark proportion or alignment, the boot-screen polarity is wrong against the firmware, "stroke weight" is claimed as an X/O cue but the geometry uses one stroke for both, and the sleep-mode table omits two real modes. Nothing is critical. One high finding, five medium.

## 1. Flow coverage — strong
Sources carry no UJ or requirement names (memlog 44 records the journeys as facilitator drafts, accepted at M53), so there is nothing to match verbatim. Both flows (E 140-163) have a named protagonist, numbered steps, a bolded climax and a failure path. Flow 1's failure (library lacks the manifest icon) matches the game-icons fallback chain; Flow 2's failure (missing custom image falls back to default) matches `SleepActivity.cpp` lines 612, 791, 801. The flows add no screens, as stated.
### Findings
- None.

## 2. Token completeness — adequate
Extracted all frontmatter tokens (10 colours, 4 typography, 21 spacing, 8 components) and every `{...}` reference in both files. All resolve; no token is orphaned. All colours have hex. The X and O hexes are the same in light and dark, and contrast is stated for both. Recomputed (WCAG relative luminance): X `#C9524D` L 0.1899, 4.38:1 on white, 4.28:1 on `#121212`, 3.09:1 on slate; O `#5A7FC4` L 0.2134, 3.99:1, 4.70:1, 3.39:1; X/O 1.098:1 (stated 1.10); drafted `#E5322D`/`#2B6CFF` L 0.191/0.185, 1.03:1. All match D 118, 126-129.
### Findings
- **[medium]** Lockup proportions are not tokenised or stated anywhere: the wordmark has no size relative to the mark (cap height, x-height, or text height as a fraction of the mark), no vertical alignment of text to the mark in the horizontal lockups, no alignment (centred or left) or line gap between "Crosshatch" and "Player" in the stacked lockup, and no minimum size for any lockup. D 31 says "Size is set per lockup, not tokenised"; D 165 defines only clear space and the mark-to-text gap. Two implementers following the spines will draw different lockups, and the spines say the mocks lose on conflict (D 102, E 57). Rated high under Component coverage (see §3); listed here because the missing values belong in `spacing`/`typography`. *Fix:* add `lockup-text-height` (as a fraction of the mark), the alignment rule, `stacked-line-gap`, and a minimum lockup/mark size.
- **[low]** Several `spacing` values are not dimensions: `mark-cell: 33.33% of the mark's width` (D 57), `lockup-clear-space`, `lockup-mark-text-gap` (D 58-59), `stacked-second-line-scale: 60%` (D 56). `typography` entries use a non-spec `note` key and carry no `fontSize` or `lineHeight` (D 28-43). *Fix:* give percentages as numbers with a base, or move the rule into prose; use `fontSize` for `device-title` (the firmware `UI_10` size) rather than a note.
- **[low]** Contrast is stated for the mark colours only. Grid-on-surface and wordmark-on-surface are load-bearing and D 129 says "the memlog records no measured figure". Recomputed: black on white 21.00:1, white on `#121212` 18.73:1. *Fix:* state the two figures.
- **[low]** "The O's hole is about 1 to 2 px" at 32 px (D 185, E 120, M18). From the geometry the inner diameter is 2 x (22 - 8) = 28 of 256 units, which is 3.5 px at 32 px (1.75 px is the radius). The unit is ambiguous. *Fix:* say radius or diameter, or quote the rasterised pixel count.

## 3. Component coverage — adequate
Every component name used (brand-mark, lockup-formal/short/stacked, sleep-boot-screen, game-icon-default-64/128) has a row in D Components (D 182-186) and E Component Patterns (E 85-92), with real rules. Names are identical across frontmatter, prose and tables. "Mark alone" and "Repo and release avatar" are placements of `brand-mark`, not separate components; that is fine.
### Findings
- **[high]** The three lockups are specified by arrangement only (D 156-161, 183). Missing: the size ratio between mark and text, the vertical alignment of text against the mark, the stacked lockup's alignment and the gap between "Crosshatch" and "Player", the minimum size, and the 1-bit/greyscale variant if any image lockup is used on a non-colour surface. The lockups are the website deliverable and this is the only visual spec for them. *Fix:* see §2 first finding; add one dimensioned lockup diagram or table to D Layout & Spacing.
- **[low]** The avatar is "the mark alone" (D 161, E 47) with no surface, padding or crop rule. The corner O rings sit about 113 units from the centre plus 30 of outer radius, 143 of 128, so a circular avatar crop would clip them. *Fix:* give the avatar a square surface with padding, or state that it is a square asset.
- **[low]** Stacked-lockup gap: D 165 and M54 say the half-cell gap between mark and "Crosshatch" is "writer's reading", not an owner decision, yet D states it as fact and E 181 says "Open Questions: None". *Fix:* confirm with the owner or list it as open.

## 4. State coverage — adequate
No new screens, so only the sleep and boot surfaces and the three icon surfaces need states. E State Patterns (E 96-106) covers icon present/absent per surface, manifest icon unknown, default/custom/cover/blank/LIGHT sleep, and boot. Checked against `SleepActivity.cpp` `onEnter` (lines 502-567) and `CrossPointSettings.h` (lines 18-27).
### Findings
- **[medium]** The sleep-mode rows omit real modes. The settings enum has `DARK, LIGHT, CUSTOM, COVER, COVER_CUSTOM, BLANK, QUICK_RESUME, TRANSPARENT_CUSTOM`. E 103-105 names "default", "custom, cover or blank" and LIGHT. QUICK_RESUME (keeps the last frame, also on timeout, lines 506-515) and TRANSPARENT_CUSTOM (keeps the framebuffer, line 527) never draw the default screen, so no mark appears. A builder cannot tell from the spine whether those are meant to be exempt. "Default sleep-screen mode" is `DARK` in code, and it is the same default branch for `LIGHT`. *Fix:* list every enum value against "mark / no mark / fallback".
- **[medium]** Boot polarity is wrong in D 175. The Polarity row sits in the table headed "the device sleep and boot screens" and says "inverted (white on black) except when the sleep-screen setting is LIGHT". `BootActivity.cpp` lines 13-19 never call `invertScreen`, so boot draws the bitmap as drawn (ink on paper), and the setting does not apply. E 90 correctly limits inversion to the sleep screen, and E 106 is silent on boot polarity, so the pair disagrees. A builder following D would invert boot. M30 also reads loosely. *Fix:* split the Polarity row: sleep inverted unless LIGHT, boot not inverted; add boot polarity to E 106.

## 5. Visual reference coverage — adequate
`mockups/`: colour-variants.html, mark-variants.html, sleep-screen.html, wordmark-variants.html. `imports/`: crosspoint-sleep-screen-photo.jpg (1500x2000, exists). All five are linked, none is orphaned, and no mock references external images. E 55-57 links all five with captions that say what each illustrates. D links the four mocks only in one bundled line (D 102), with a second inline link for the sleep mock (D 184) and one for mark-variants (D 143). Spine-wins-on-conflict is stated in D 102, E 22 and E 57, once per file plus a repeat.
### Findings
- **[medium]** `mockups/sleep-screen.html` shows the superseded design: heading "stacked short-name lockup (L3)", Noto Sans Bold 18 pt title (38.35 px), small line at title top + 51, and icons at 120/160/200/240. The decision (M33, D 178) is the stock layout with Ubuntu 10 bold and +25. D 184 says the mock "shows candidate sizes" but not that its title font and small-line offset are wrong for every size, including the 120 px one. E 57 gives no caveat at all and calls it "the device layout". A builder who opens the mock for the 120 px case gets the wrong title font. *Fix:* put the caveat in E 57 (title font and +51 offset superseded), or re-render the 120 px case with the stock values.
- **[low]** D has no inline link at Colors (D 122 cites "the 2026-10-03 colour page" without a link), at Typography/Lockups (wordmark-variants), or for the import photo (D "stock layout" section; the photo is linked only in E 55 and in `sources`). *Fix:* add inline links in D Colors, Layout lockups and the sleep table.

## 6. Bloat & overspecification — adequate
Mostly proportionate. D Brand & Style, Colors and the Do's table carry editorial voice, which is allowed. The weight is in repeats and non-decisions.
### Findings
- **[low]** The same numbers are stated up to three times: mark geometry in frontmatter, the D 147-150 table and the path string; 120/70/95 in D 171-173, E 39-40 and E 128; contrast figures in D 126, D 129 and E 119. Drift risk, no added information. *Fix:* keep one canonical place and reference it.
- **[low]** E "Implementation notes for builders" (E 165-177) is repo fact, not a design decision, and carries line numbers that will rot (619-632, 13-19, 612/791/801). I checked them against the repo today and they are correct, and the `STR_CROSSPOINT`/`Logo120` "used only by these two activities" claim holds. The ledger note is useful for the builder, but belongs in a ticket. *Fix:* drop the line numbers, or move the section out of the spine.
- **[low]** D 178 (superseded sizes), D 118/131 (slate and pastel rejects) and the last half of D 129 are decision history, and the Do's table (D 192-203) restates Colors and Typography almost row for row. D 136 and E 120 justify "Player" at 14 px in 1-bit, but no 1-bit lockup is specified (the lockups are website and image only), so the check is untied to a surface. *Fix:* trim to the rule plus one line of reason.

## 7. Inheritance discipline — strong
`sources` frontmatter: all 12 repo paths resolve (checked each: memlog, the 2026-10-01 DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md, `SleepActivity.cpp`, `BootActivity.cpp`, `Logo120.h`, `english.yaml`, `game-icons.md`, `upstream-touches.md`, the photo; plus `NotoSans-Bold.ttf`, `GameRowIcon.h` and `FilesPage.html`, cited in prose). `STR_CROSSPOINT`, `STR_SLEEPING`, `STR_BOOTING` exist in `english.yaml` lines 6-8. The firmware facts match the code (draw positions, fonts, `h/2+70`, `h/2+95`, version at `h-30`). The title-screen/hand-off fallback chain matches the 2026-10-01 spine (480 x 480 band, `handoff.png` else `title.png` else icon at 128). Component and surface names agree across both files and with the games spine. `upstream-touches.md` lists no entry for the sleep/boot files, `Logo120.h` or `FilesPage.html`, as E 170-171 says.
### Findings
- **[low]** E 28 says the devices are "800 x 480 panels" and the sleep/boot canvas is portrait 480 x 800; the games spine (2026-10-01 E 33) calls the device 480 x 800 portrait throughout, and the code centres on `getScreenWidth()`/`getScreenHeight()` for the current orientation. The 480 x 800 and "the panel is 800 x 480" wording (D 174, E 28) will confuse a reader. *Fix:* use the games spine's wording and say once that the layout is relative to the screen.

## 8. Shape fit — strong
D order is canonical: Brand & Style, Colors, Typography, Layout & Spacing, Components, Do's and Don'ts. Elevation & Depth and Shapes are omitted, which is defensible for a mark (round caps and joins are in the geometry). E has all eight required defaults (Foundation, IA, Voice and Tone, Component Patterns, State Patterns, Interaction Primitives, Accessibility Floor, Key Flows), and Interaction Primitives is a legitimate "None" with a ban list. Responsive & Platform is present and fits the three surfaces. The invented "Implementation notes" and "Open Questions" are the only extras (see §6).
### Findings
- **[low]** No Inspiration section, although the memlog names reference and reject sources: Phosphor (the construction to match, M15, M20), CrossPoint's own logo (the look not required, M11), chalk/pastel (rejected, M37-39). The material is in D Brand & Style and Colors, so nothing is lost, but the rubric asks for it when references exist. *Fix:* add a three-line Inspiration section, or note in E Foundation that D carries it.

## Mechanical notes
- Frontmatter: both complete (`name`, `status`, `created`, `updated`, `sources`; D has `description`, E has `design`). `sources` identical except E adds `Logo120.h` and the i18n keys.
- All `{token}` references resolve in both files; no unused tokens. No Mermaid. No broken inline links: the four mocks and the import resolve from the spine directory.
- Geometry check against the A2 symbol (`mockups/mark-variants.html`): grid `M88 24V232 M168 24V232 M24 88H232 M24 168H232` matches D 147; rings `cx/cy 48 and 208, r 22` match; X diagonals `110-146` and `190-226` are centre ± 18 as stated. Board (O top-left, X centre, X bottom-right, O top-right) matches D 152. The ring's outer edge is at 78 and the neighbouring grid stroke's edge at 80, a 2-unit gap that is under 1 px at 120 px and about 0.5 px at 64 px in 1-bit; the O can fuse into the grid there. **[medium]** The spines say 64 px and 120 px were "checked and not flagged"/"clear" (E 120) but never say how the 120 and 64 px bitmaps are produced from the SVG (threshold, hinting, hand-corrected pixels). Only the 128 px icon is specified as native (D 186). A builder will rasterise differently and the ring/grid fusion risk is real at these sizes. *Fix:* state the derivation, and record the rendered ring-to-grid gap in pixels at 120, 64 and 128.
- The D 194 and D 182 "shape and weight" claim, repeated at E 118 ("a cross and a ring differ by shape and by stroke weight"), is not supported by the geometry: X and O share stroke width 16 in the A2 symbol. **[medium]** *Fix:* drop "weight" from all three places, or specify a weight difference in the geometry.
- Memlog completeness: every decision at M6-M54 that is not superseded appears in the pair. The one soft item is M34 (Ubuntu title "to confirm with user"), confirmed at M40 and reflected in D 138.
