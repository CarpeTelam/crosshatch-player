Hand-off and Result

The hidden pass match between seats. Each screen has one button: the Result banner at the bottom, "I'm ready" in the middle, so a double tap on one cannot land on the other. Only a tap on the button, or Confirm, moves on; a tap elsewhere does nothing. Nothing of one seat's frame reaches the next seat. Not drawn: the forced exit (sleep or Replace), a plain white screen with no icon and no text, then the user's CrossPoint sleep screen. X4 Pro, 480 x 800 at 1x, 1-bit: ink #000000 and paper #FFFFFF only. Icons are drawn approximations: the game-controller stands for the game's own icon. The spines win over this mock.
