Title screen

The game's title screen as designed: the header keeps the game's name and back arrow, the 480 x 480 splash band (y = 94 to 574) shows the game's icon at 128 px centred in it (or the game's title.png, at most 480 x 480), and the menu rows follow directly under it: 226 px, room for three two-line rows (3 x 74 px) and no more. X4 Pro, 480 x 800 at 1x, 1-bit: ink #000000 and paper #FFFFFF only; the selection is the 25 % dither. Icons are drawn approximations of the Phosphor library icons named. The spines win over this mock.

Pass open (solo and pass) with a Valid save: Continue / "Load the previous game" first and selected, New game naming the current mode (the last started, here Solo), then Options (one line, no second line), shown because the host can start more than one of its modes. The third row ends at y = 798, inside the screen. A solo-only game with no settings, such as Counter, shows no Options row; a game with settings would read "Solo · <value>".
