# D5 round 1 digest: Sticky and Boox Picco display stacks vs X4 Pro

Researcher: 18 tool calls, 12 sources. Corrections to the brief: the "Sticky" is not the reTerminal E1001/E1002 family (E1001 is a 7.5" UC8179 panel without touch); the reTerminal Sticky is a separate 3.97" touch product on ESP32-S3R8. ## Key excerpts
- Seeed wiki: `#define BOARD_SCREEN_COMBO 520 // reTerminal E1001 (UC8179)`.
- Seeed_GFX `Setup515_Seeed_XIAO_EPaper_3inch97.h`: `#define SSD1677_DRIVER` / `#define TFT_WIDTH 800` / `#define TFT_HEIGHT 480` / `#define EPD_HORIZONTAL_MIRROR` (identical in `Setup506_Seeed_XIAO_EPaper_4inch26.h`).
- Seeed_GFX `SSD1677_Init.h`: `writecommand(0x3C); writedata(0x05); // Set border waveform` ... `writecommand(0x18); writedata(0x80);` ... `writecommand(0x11); writedata(0x03);`.
- ESPHome docs PR #7463: "Seeed-reTerminal-Sticky-Gray4, a 4-level grayscale mode with full refresh only ... partial updates are black and white, and the first one removes the gray levels from the whole panel until the next full update."
- Aftermath: "Text looks slightly clearer than the X4" ... "the X3, X3 Pro, and X4 Pro all dropped the port in favor of a POGO pin connector."
- Notebookcheck: "Onyx has not yet confirmed any further details regarding the Boox Picco's specifications, price, or availability."

## Leads
- ESPHome `epaper_spi` SSD1677 Sticky model source (esphome#18931, #19213, #19365): the most direct public record of the Sticky's gray4 LUT and 0x22 sequences, and of why partial-in-gray was rejected.
- Seeed_GFX `SSD1677_Defines.h` lines 38-130 and 212-400 in the clone for exact 0x22 operands, 0x32 LUT bytes, 0x26 old-plane usage and `EPD_SET_TEMP`.
- Good Display 3.97" 800x480 part number to confirm whether Sticky and X4 Pro differ only in diagonal.
- Boox Picco: re-check after the November 2026 launch for FCC filings and teardowns.
