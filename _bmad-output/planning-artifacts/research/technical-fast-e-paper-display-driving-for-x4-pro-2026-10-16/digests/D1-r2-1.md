## Leads
- The SDK code was only ever referenced, never pushed: 26 minutes separate the pointer to ec2857f and its revert. Asking hellominecraft913-dev (an issue on Ink-boy) is the only route to the actual driver.
- "stock X3 UC8279 bank" implies the DU LUT is lifted from the Xteink X3's own waveform bank; uxjulia's `Uc8253X3Luts.h` and upstream `Uc8279X4Driver.cpp` are the closest public starting points.
- Forks pages 2-5 of Free-Ink/freeink-sdk unchecked; GitHub code search needs login.
