# D2 round 2 digest: InkBoy's dither-free 4-shade grayscale model (deep read)

Researcher: code-only read of the InkBoy clone at 2673c8a (scratchpad `kuslota/InkBoy`, root `R`), no web calls. Publisher kuslota, pub_date 2026-10-12, accessed 2026-10-16 for every claim.

## Findings

Q1 physical idea
- claim: The controller's two image planes select a LUT row per pixel; InkBoy defines (R,BW) 00=HOLD, 10=BLACK, 01=WHITE, and in weak mode 11=SPECIAL. The black mask is written to the "previous" (R) plane, the white mask to the BW plane, then activation 0x0C. | source: R/components/ssd1681_fast/include/ssd1681_fast.h:41-42; ssd1681_fast.c:610-620; include/ssd1681_gray_waveform.h:16-17 | confidence: high | class: code-fact
- claim: The waveform is the Waveshare V2 partial LUT patched to one active frame: byte 1 (row 00, group 1) and byte 37 (row 11, group 1) zeroed, byte 60 (G0 TP) = 0, byte 68 (G1 phase B) = 0 when frames==1, byte 144 low nibble = frame-rate code 7. Stock G1 phase-A selectors are 0x80 (VSL) for row 01/white and 0x40 (VSH1) for row 10/black. | source: ssd1681_fast.c:359-376, 480-489, 503-519; ssd1681_vendor_tables.h:21-35 | confidence: high (byte-to-row reading is the researcher's; row/VSL assignments are the author's comments) | class: code-fact
- claim: A "dose" is one activation of that one-frame waveform (~14 ms in grayscale); a mid-gray is reached by issuing N doses toward the endpoint, then holding (00). The per-pixel level is a model (0..192, shade x 64), advanced per delivered dose by `level += (target*64-level)/remaining` so the last dose lands exactly on target. | source: R/docs/how-it-works.md:29,57; R/main/gray_plan.c:5-19, 239-246 | confidence: high | class: code-fact
- claim: Follow-up scan in the same slot: a pixel drives again only if its transition's HOLD bit is off and `remaining > 1`; otherwise the second scan sends HOLD (00) for it, which "ends the pulse promptly and suits the gray targets". | source: gray_plan.c:186-189; gray_plan.h:40-42; fast_display.c:313-331; how-it-works.md:74 | confidence: high | class: design-pattern
- claim: Voltages: VSL presets 0x32/-15 V (stock), 0x2a/-13 V, 0x22/-11 V, 0x1a/-9 V; VSH2 0xb0/5.8 V default, 0x2d/11 V or 0xce/8.8 V in weak modes; VSH1, VCOM, gate and EOPT stay stock. | source: ssd1681_fast.c:381-394; ssd1681_gray_waveform.h:21-25; ssd1681_vendor_tables.h:34-35 | confidence: high | class: code-fact

Q2 per-pixel model
- claim: `gray_pixel_t` is 2 bytes: `level` (u8), `remaining:4`, `target:2`, `source:2`; 40,000 of them = 80,000 B, plus `active[5000]`, `previous_binary[5000]`, `previous_game[5760]`, `weak_wl[5000]`; allocated in internal RAM first with PSRAM fallback. History is a separate 3-byte `{origin, black_steps, white_steps}` x 40,000 = 120,000 B in PSRAM, allocated only when HISTORY != OFF. | source: gray_plan.h:7-31; fast_display.c:43-48, 206-212 | confidence: high | class: code-fact
- claim: A 20 KB `pulse_level[4][193][13][2]` table precomputes one- and two-dose level steps. The parallel planner copies policy tables into an internal-RAM context and runs rows 0..99 on the display task and 100..199 on a worker pinned to core 1; single-core fallback; results identical by construction and by test. | source: gray_plan.c:5-7, 39-47; gray_plan_parallel.c:13-24, 306-335, 369-399; how-it-works.md:91-93 | confidence: high | class: code-fact
- claim: The planner re-walks only dirty rows (25-byte binary row or 40-byte packed game row changed); the game viewport is 160x144 at (20,28), packed 2 bits per pixel, target = 3 - value. | source: gray_plan.c:112-139 | confidence: high | class: code-fact

Q3 doses and history
- claim: Retarget: base dose = linear interpolation between dose[low][target] and dose[high][target] by `level%64`, rounded up; if the pixel is mid-transition the base is scaled by `retarget_percent` (clamped 1..12); `source` is frozen to the nearest shade. | source: gray_plan.c:53-56, 84-88, 96-108 | confidence: high | class: code-fact
- claim: History route = (origin == new target) with `via` = abandoned target; six routes LKL, DKD, DWD, LWL, DLD, LDL. Completed return: budget = base + return_delta, clamped 1..12. Reversal mid-transition: budget = base x reverse_percent/100. Origin is recorded only when settled and the new target is K or W (or the DLD/LDL routes are non-neutral). | source: gray_plan.c:57-79; gray_adaptive.h:5-38; tuning.md:66-84 | confidence: high | class: code-fact
- claim: Weak W>L: eligibility frozen at retarget when target==L, previous target==W, settled at level 192; at scan time eligible black-mask bits go to the SPECIAL row (VSH2 11 V or 8.8 V) instead of VSH1. Docs: "can fix a dark stain behind a moving white object"; weaker takes longer. | source: gray_plan.c:80-83; fast_display.c:254-268; tuning.md:71 | confidence: high | class: code-fact

## Re-implementation checklist (author's terms)
1. Volatile LUT (never OTP): take the vendor partial LUT, zero row 00 (HOLD) and row 11 in the active group, keep G1 phase A as the single frame (TP=1), zero G0 and G1-B, set the fastest frame-rate code; rows: 01 to VSL (white), 10 to VSH1 (black), 11 to VSH2 (weak black).
2. Keep analog power on between scans; write both planes every scan; disjoint masks; fast SPI.
3. Per-pixel 2-byte state {level 0..192, remaining:4, target:2, source:2} plus an active bitmap and previous-image copies; optional 3-byte history.
4. 12-entry dose table plus a 16-bit HOLD mask; interpolate the dose by current level; retarget scaling for interrupted pixels; freeze `source`.
5. Mask pass: lighten to white else black; follow-up only if HOLD off and remaining > 1. Commit only after acknowledged delivery, 1 or 2 doses by direction match.
6. Scheduler: fixed 33.334 ms image phase, drive-period floor (30 ms in pair mode), scan-cost high-water estimate, completion scan only when it fits, else the new image wins; HOLD-ending scan after the last drive.
7. History routes (origin and step counters, return delta, reversal percent) and the weak W>L special row.
8. Caps and cleaning: partial counter with a forced full refresh after N; full refresh on mode change, entry and exit.
9. Split planner rows across cores with an internal-RAM working set; keep a frozen reference implementation and differential host tests.
10. Calibrate with a transition-test screen (4 bands x 3 sweeping rectangles); export defaults to a JSON preset.
