## Controller-level vs panel-specific
- Controller-level (portable to any UC8179/UC8279, and by analogy SSD1677 with its own LUT format): keep the old frame in DTM1, write the new to DTM2, run a differential refresh with a LUT whose WW and BB cells are not idle but apply a short, weak same-polarity pulse, so unchanged pixels get reinforced on every page turn and the periodic full flash becomes unnecessary; the windowed variant for local settle passes; the no-op fast settle after every full; the strong scrub on the page after a grayscale image; the split DRF start/finish wait.
- Panel-specific: every LUT byte (recovered from Xteink stock firmware V5.6.33 for the UC8253, decompiled from the UC8279d init for the newer X3), the CDI values, and the E0/E5 settings on UC8279d; the author's SDK keeps the X4 Pro's UC8279 variant as a separate driver with its own PSR/PLL.
- Caveats documented: none on temperature, lifetime, or panel variance; hardware-validation gaps noted; full sync re-powers the charge pump; white pixels drift toward gray in long standby (fixed with an extra settle pass). The UC8179 header says that controller's MTP holds temperature-compensated LUTs and the driver uses PSR REG=0 there.
