## Leads
- PaperBoy (https://gitlab.com/zephray/paperboy) is the real origin of per-pixel driving; it drives raw panel signals, so its applicability to SSD1677/UC8179 is via InkBoy's "plane bits as LUT row selector" adaptation.
- The mask-selector trick depends on the controller exposing 2 image planes feeding a 4-row LUT (SSD168x family). Check whether SSD1677 (800x480) and UC8179/UC8279 offer the same RAM0/RAM1 -> LUT-row mapping and a volatile LUT command; this is the gating question for the decision.
- Scaling: 40,000 pixels needed a two-core planner and internal-RAM working sets; 384,000 pixels is 9.6x more, and the bit-plane counter store scales to 240 KB (4 planes x 48 KB) for dithered mode alone.
- Lifetime cap (2,402 partials, clean every 2,400) and the OTP-command whitelist are safety patterns worth copying.
- `docs/tuning.md` and `tools/test_*.py` (host-fake test suite, including `test_gray_plan_equivalence.py`) show the author's verification approach; not read in depth.
- Author channel "Bo Made It" may have a follow-up video; YouTube page was blocked by a captcha this run.
