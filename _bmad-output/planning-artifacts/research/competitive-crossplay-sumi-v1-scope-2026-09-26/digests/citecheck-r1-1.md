### Wrong number or contradicted by the source

Several are dice or card games, not board games. — suggested fix: "The other eight Play Nearby games are solo, against the computer, or Nearby only [3] (confidence: medium, grep-based)".
- [18] "The last Play Nearby commits in the fetched history are from 2026-09-04" — problem: on the clone, `git log -- src/apps_local/link` shows commits dated 2026-09-12 (Go joining Play Nearby, which touched LinkPlay.h) and 2026-09-05. Also, the date came from the clone's git log, not the releases page that [18] names. — suggested fix: "The last link-layer commits in the fetched history are from 2026-09-12, when Go was added to Play Nearby (clone git log)".
- [69] "listed in the 3.3.0 release, which is built on ESP-IDF 5.5" and the Open questions row "ESP-NOW v2 (1,470 B payloads) needs Arduino-ESP32 3.3.0 or later with IDF 5.5 [69]" — problem: PR #11524 already appears in the release notes of Arduino-ESP32 3.2.1 (2025-07-03). The 3.3.0 notes list it again. verify-r1 records the milestone as 3.2.1, and the IDF side of v2 arrived in 5.4. — suggested fix: "first shipped in Arduino-ESP32 3.2.1 (2025-07-03). ESP-IDF v2 support starts at 5.4, and payloads were 1,490 B before 5.4.2". Change the open question to "needs Arduino-ESP32 ≥3.2.1".
