# Two devices play one match over ESP-NOW

## Description

Adds Play Nearby: the wire protocol and reliable link in `GameCore`, tested over a lossy fake link, the ESP-NOW adapter and link task, a UDP link so two simulators play (2026-10-12), the host lobby and guest join, and clean endings when a peer leaves or goes silent. It measures the spec's open radio questions. With epic-first-party-games's games in `games/<id>/`, it adds `nearby` to Ultimate tic-tac-toe's and Battleship's manifests and plays their nearby rounds (owner, 2026-10-04).

## Outcome

Two players each on their own device finish a round of an unmodified game; the nearby part of CAP-5, the peer-left part of CAP-7, and CAP-10's nearby rounds are the signal.

## Requirements

This epic owns the Play Nearby part of CAP-5, the peer-left part of CAP-7, and CAP-10's nearby rounds (Ultimate tic-tac-toe and Battleship; epic-first-party-games owns the rest of CAP-10). Written at inception, 2026-10-11; lines without a CAP id cite their source. Where an owner Decision later supersedes a line, the line is edited in the same commit (`docs/crosshatch/orchestrated-epics.md`).

- R1 (CAP-5; spine AD-1, AD-13; owner Decision 2026-10-10, transport-agnostic link): `lib/GameCore` has one link interface (`ILink`, AD-1's port) that `FakeLink`, `EspNowLink`, and the simulator's `UdpLink` (R16) implement. It sends and receives opaque frames and names a peer by an opaque handle, never a MAC address; broadcast is a separate call. `GameCore::Protocol` is the only encoder and decoder of AD-13's frame and every payload (`ADVERT`, `JOIN`, `ACCEPT`, `STATE`, `ABORT`, `PING`, and `ACK`, an empty unsequenced frame type of its own (owner Decision 2026-10-12, entry 2's checkpoint, Q2); `MOVE` and `REJECT` left the set on 2026-10-12, R4), with AD-13's layout and length limits otherwise unchanged, and it refuses a short, oversized, or malformed frame, a wrong magic or `proto`, and another `session`. `FakeLink` lives in `test/game_core/` and drops, delays, duplicates, and reorders frames from a seed, so a run repeats exactly.
- R2 (CAP-5, CAP-7; AD-13; owner Decision 2026-10-10): `GameCore::ReliableLink` alone owns `seq` and `ack`. Reliable frames reach the session once each and in order; `ack` is cumulative; `ADVERT`, `PING`, `ACK`, and `ABORT` consume no `seq` (an `ABORT` reaches the session whatever is still unacked, so a leaver's last unacked `STATE` cannot hold it back); an unacked `STATE` may be replaced by a newer one, except one that ends a round (`over`), which is kept until acked. The resend interval and the peer-silence limit are `ReliableLink` settings that default to AD-13's 400 ms and 10 s, never literals elsewhere. A peer silent for the limit is reported once. Time comes from `IClock`, so the suites run without waiting. Only a frame the session has no room for is left unacked, and the peer's resend delivers it later (R5); a `STATE` the session refuses by the adopt rule (R4) is still acked, or its sender would resend it forever.
- R3 (CAP-5; AD-11, AD-13, AD-15, AD-19; inception Decision Q2a): the lobby handshake is `GameCore` code over `ILink`. The host draws the `session` id from `IRandom` when its lobby opens and broadcasts `ADVERT` with the game id, package hash, levels, surface, seats, and a host label it is given as opaque bytes by the radio layer. A guest keeps the hosts it hears for the game it opened, each with a verdict: joinable, or a different version (package hash, or `surface` while either level is a preview; a host on another `proto` is refused by `Protocol` and never listed, owner Decision 2026-10-12, entry 2's checkpoint, Q1). The host gives seats in `JOIN` arrival order (host seat 1, the guest seat 2), answers an incompatible `JOIN` with `ABORT(version_mismatch)` and a late one with `ABORT(full)`, starts only with at least 2 seats filled, then sends `ACCEPT {seat, n}` to every guest and stops `ADVERT`. `ctx.api` is the lowest `hostApi` in the roster. A cancel on either side sends `ABORT(left)`. Before Start, a joined guest that cancels or goes silent frees its seat and the host waits again; a host that cancels or goes silent is reported to its waiting guest (owner, 2026-10-12). Nothing in it assumes n = 2; a v1 host's maximum is 2 (`HostCaps.maxSeats`).
- R4 (CAP-5; AD-8, AD-9, AD-11, AD-13, AD-17 as amended 2026-10-12; owner Decision 2026-10-12, after reviewing crossplay `0a1d17f` (`https://github.com/ma-r-s/crossplay`, MIT)'s `LinkSession`): a nearby match runs as pass-and-play does, one local seat per device. Each device runs `Session` as the authority for its own seat: on its turn it calls `input` and `apply` locally; a rejected move reaches its `input` as `{kind = "rejected", reason}` and never leaves the device; an accepted move ships the whole `STATE {ver, turn, over, winners, snapshot}`. The other device adopts a `STATE` only from its peer, only when its `ver` is newer, and only when the turn in its own current status (from the last `STATE` it applied or adopted) is the peer's seat, or when seat 1 starts a round (this device's status is over, or it has no state yet); it adopts as a mid-match restore inside `Session` on the VM task, and gates input and the end-of-round flow on the shipped status. Nothing outside `Session` tracks whose turn it is, and the VM adopts only between one `input`-to-`apply` cycle and the next, so a local move is never applied to a state the peer has already moved on from (crossplay's `submit` refusal). A `STATE` that ends a round is never replaced by a later one, so the other device always reaches Over and gets the `over` event once. Until seat 1's first `STATE` arrives, the other device shows that it is waiting for the host and takes no taps. Only the turn holder advances `ver` (and seat 1 when it starts a round), which never resets for the life of the `session` id, rematches included. Seat 1's device runs `setup` (with its settings) and Play again. `MOVE` and `REJECT` are not on the wire. `ch.store` writes made in `apply` land on the mover's device. A nearby match saves nothing (no `resume.bin`). The rule is written for two seats; more would need the host to relay `STATE` between guests (spine Deferred).
- R5 (CAP-7; AD-18, AD-20, AD-21; owner Decisions 2026-10-04; inception Decision Q1): the session queue from the `GameLink` task to the match is 4 entries deep, each a PSRAM handle. The policy below is `GameCore` code over injected storage with an overflow counter (`lib/GameCore` has no allocator or log); `src/games` gives it the PSRAM entries, the cross-task handoff, and the `LINK` log line. A newer `STATE` replaces a queued one, unless the queued one ends a round (`over`), which stays until the match consumes it; a frame that finds the queue full is left unacked (R2), so the peer resends it after the resend interval and nothing is lost; each overflow logs one `LINK` line with a running count. A lost peer and a received `ABORT` never enter the queue: they set a one-shot latched slot (its reason, the first one wins) that the match reads on its next `loop()`, so neither is ever dropped. The `GameLink` task runs the pump, the resends, and the silence check, never takes `RenderLock`, and never touches activity state. The move to `PeerGone` waits for the match's next `loop()`, so it waits behind an open overlay.
- R6 (CAP-7; AD-14, AD-20, AD-21; inception Decision Q3): a match ends cleanly on both devices. Leave flushes the link's unacked frames for at most 800 ms, then sends `ABORT(left)` three times, before the VM stops and the radio goes down; a forced exit sends `ABORT(left)` three times without flushing, as crossplay sends its `Bye`, so the peer learns at once unless every copy is lost. A script error sends `ABORT(script_error)` and shows the error view. From every non-terminal state an `ABORT` or the silence limit moves the match to `PeerGone`, whose "player left" view has one reason line: the other player left, went silent, hit a game error, or runs a different version; Back leaves. Only the host offers Play again; a guest's end-of-round menu shows "waiting for host" and Leave. The lobby and the match set `preventAutoSleep`.
- R7 (CAP-5, CAP-7; AD-2, AD-18; owner Decision 2026-10-10): `src/games` holds everything radio-specific. `EspNowLink` implements `ILink` over ESP-NOW v2 on fixed channel 1 with no encryption, brought up in AD-18's order on the STA interface, hardened as crossplay `0a1d17f` (`https://github.com/ma-r-s/crossplay`, MIT)'s `LinkRadio::begin` is: auto-reconnect off before the disconnect, the disconnect waited out before the channel is set (a channel set while associated is silently reverted), the broadcast peer registered, teardown flagged as soon as `esp_now_init` succeeds so every failure path tears down, `end()` safe to call twice, and modem power save with a 50 ms wake window; its receive callback only copies frames into a ring buffer allocated at bring-up, never static, and calls no `GameCore` code; only `ADVERT` is broadcast. A precondition probe answers whether Wi-Fi is up (any `WiFi.getMode()` but off, which covers the web server) and whether free internal heap is under 100 KB. `NearbySession` and the `GameLink` task (priority 1, core 1, 4 KB stack) are written against `ILink`, so the host harness runs them over `FakeLink`; only the wiring that hands them an `EspNowLink` is radio code. The radio is off after leaving the lobby or the match. Under `SIMULATOR`, `EspNowLink` is compiled out and the UDP link (R16) carries nearby. A story that needs a radio fact in `GameCore` stops for the owner.

The lobby sets `preventAutoSleep` while open. Cancel or Back tears the radio down and returns to the title screen. Start moves the session into `GameMatchActivity` with Replace.
